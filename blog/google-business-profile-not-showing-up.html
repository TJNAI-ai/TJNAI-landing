Google Business Profile Not Showing Up in Search? What to Check First

By TJNAI · November 3, 2026 · 9 min read

If your Google Business Profile isn't showing up, check four things in order: whether it's verified, whether it's new or was just edited, whether it's been suspended, and whether there's a duplicate. Google says only verified businesses show on Maps and Search. If your profile does show up for your name but not for other searches, that's a ranking problem, not a broken profile. Every check below links to Google's own help pages, so you can check the source yourself.

First, check whether your profile is missing or just not ranking

Start by finding your own profile the way Google says to. Google says "to find your profile, you must sign in to the Google Account associated with it" (Google Business Profile Help). Then:

  1. Sign in to the Google Account you use to manage your profile.
  2. On Google Search, search for your business's name and city, or search for "my business."
  3. On Google Maps, search for your business's name and city.

What you find tells you which kind of problem you have. We split it two ways, which is our own framing rather than a Google term. If your profile is missing for everyone, work through Checks 1 to 4 below. If it shows up when you search your name but not for a search like "plumber near me," your profile isn't broken. Skip to the section on ranking.

We also check what the public sees by searching on a phone that isn't signed in to the business's Google Account. That's our own practice, not a Google step.

Check 1: Is your business verified?

Verification comes first, because Google says "only verified businesses can show their business info on Maps and Search" (Google Business Profile Help). Our Google Business Profile audit checklist starts with the same check.

You don't get to pick how you verify. Google says "verification methods are automatically determined by Google and can't be changed," and the options depend on "your business type, public info, region, or business hours" (Google Business Profile Help).

Verification is still pending

A pending verification usually just needs time. Google says "verification reviews take up to 5 business days. On rare occasions, reviews may take longer" (Google Business Profile Help).

Some profiles skip the wait. Google says "in some cases, Google verifies a profile instantly," for example when "you verify your website with Google Search Console" (Google Business Profile Help). That only happens in some cases, so don't count on it.

You're waiting on a postcard

If Google is mailing you a code, Google says "most codes arrive within 14 days" (Google Business Profile Help). While you wait:

  1. Don't edit your business name, address or category. Google says "if you do, the code we sent you won't work."
  2. Don't ask for a new code. Google says "do not request a new code."
  3. Enter the code once it arrives. Google says "verification codes expire after 30 days."

All three come from Google's verification help page (Google Business Profile Help).

The "Get verified" button came back

A returning button means verification didn't finish. Google says "when the 'Get verified' button appears on your Business Profile again, it means Google couldn't completely verify your business" (Google Business Profile Help).

Verifying by phone when you have a phone menu

If Google verifies you by phone, the code can't reach an automated phone menu. Google says "Interactive Voice Response (IVR) systems won't get the code" (Google Business Profile Help). You need to be able to answer the business phone or receive texts.

During verification, make sure the call reaches a live phone or a direct line, not a phone menu.

Nobody from Google will ask for your code

Keep your code to yourself. Google says "Google will never ask for your verification code" (Google Business Profile Help). If someone asks you for it, that isn't Google.

Check 2: Is your profile new or recently edited?

A new or just-edited profile can take a while to show. Google says "rankings for new businesses can take up to a month to appear in search results," and edits "can take up to 3 days to appear" (Google Business Profile Help).

Check 3: Is your profile suspended or disabled?

A suspended profile is hidden from the public. Google says "if your Business Profile violates our guidelines, it becomes disabled or suspended and won't show to other users on Google until you resolve the issue" (Google Business Profile Help).

Google tells you when it happens: "if your business is suspended, you'll get a notification in the Google Account you use to manage your profile" (Google Business Profile Help). When Google removes a profile, "the public can't go to the profile." When a whole account is restricted, "the Business Profiles you manage are suspended and you won't be able to create or claim other profiles" (Google Business Profile Help).

Breaking Google's guidelines can get a profile suspended. Here are three rules the guidelines spell out (Google Business Profile Help):

  • Extra words in your business name. Google says "including unnecessary information in your business name isn't permitted, and could result in the suspension of your Business Profile."
  • A P.O. box or virtual office as your address. Google says "P.O. boxes or mailboxes located at remote locations aren't acceptable," and a rented mailing address you don't operate from, "also known as a virtual office," isn't eligible for a Business Profile.
  • More than one profile for the same business. See Check 4.

How to appeal a suspension

Fix the problem, then appeal on the same profile. Don't make a new profile, and remember you only get one appeal. Google says "do not create a new Business Profile for the same business," and "appeals can only be submitted once" (Google Business Profile Help).

Because you only get one appeal, gather your evidence first. Google lists optional evidence such as official business registration, a business license, tax certificates and business utility bills. Google also says to "check that the business name and address match the profile you want to make an appeal for" (Google Business Profile Help).

Check 4: Do you have a duplicate profile?

A duplicate can confuse how your business shows up. Google says "there should only be one profile per business, as this can cause problems with how your information displays on Google Maps and Search" (Google Business Profile Help). Google also says "do not create more than one page for each location of your business, either in a single account or multiple accounts" (Google Business Profile Help).

Service-area businesses: why you don't see a pin

If you travel to your customers, Google asks you to hide your address. Google says "if you're a service-area business, you should hide your business address from customers. For example, if you're a plumber and run your business from your residential address, clear the address from your Business Profile" (Google Business Profile Help).

Many home-service businesses work this way, such as cleaning, junk removal or pressure washing.

You can still have a profile without a storefront. Google says "if your business either has a physical location that customers can visit, or travels to customers where they are, you can create a Business Profile on Google" (Google Business Profile Help). So if you hid your address as Google asks, it's expected that customers don't see a street address. Google adds that it "determines how best to display your business address based on your business information and information from other sources" (Google Business Profile Help).

It shows up, just not for the searches you want

If your profile shows for your name but not for other searches, the next thing to look at is how complete and accurate it is. Google says "businesses with complete and accurate info are more likely to show up in local search results. If your business info isn't accurate, your Business Profile might not show up for relevant searches in your area" (Google Business Profile Help).

Google says "local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and popularity," and its help page names the factors relevance, distance and prominence. "Distance refers to how far each business is from the customer who's searching" (Google Business Profile Help).

You can't buy your way up. Google says "there's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google" (Google Business Profile Help).

My reviews aren't showing

Missing reviews often have a simple reason. Google says possible reasons include policy checks that "might take a few days," a recent profile merge, and customers using older phones or software (Google Business Profile Help).

Some reviews don't come back. Google says "reviews removed for policy violations won't be restored" (Google Business Profile Help). And if your profile was suspended, Google says "sometimes reviews might be removed after a Business Profile is reinstated. If this happens, contact support for assistance" (Google Business Profile Help).

Frequently asked questions

Why is my Google Business Profile not showing up after verification?

Your verification may not be finished, or your profile may still be new. Google says verification reviews "take up to 5 business days," and a returning "Get verified" button means Google "couldn't completely verify your business" (Google Business Profile Help). Rankings for new businesses "can take up to a month to appear in search results" (Google Business Profile Help).

How long does it take for a new Google Business Profile to show up?

Google says verification reviews "take up to 5 business days," and "on rare occasions, reviews may take longer" (Google Business Profile Help). Google also says "rankings for new businesses can take up to a month to appear in search results" (Google Business Profile Help).

How do I know if my Google Business Profile is suspended?

Google sends a notice: "you'll get a notification in the Google Account you use to manage your profile." A suspended profile "won't show to other users on Google until you resolve the issue" (Google Business Profile Help).

Should I create a new profile if mine is suspended?

No. Google says "do not create a new Business Profile for the same business" (Google Business Profile Help), and "there should only be one profile per business" (Google Business Profile Help).

Why doesn't my service-area business show an address or pin on Google Maps?

Google asks service-area businesses to hide their address from customers, and says it "determines how best to display your business address" (Google Business Profile Help). You can still have a profile if you travel to customers (Google Business Profile Help).

Why are my Google reviews not showing?

Reviews can be delayed by policy checks that "might take a few days," a profile merge, or a customer's older phone or software. Reviews "removed for policy violations won't be restored" (Google Business Profile Help).

Can I pay Google to show my profile higher?

No. Google says "there's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google" (Google Business Profile Help).
