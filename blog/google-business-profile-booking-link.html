How to Add a Booking Link to Your Google Business Profile (and Keep Google From Removing It)

By TJNAI · November 3, 2026 · 8 min read

To add a booking link to your Google Business Profile, open your verified profile on Google Search and select Booking, or open Google Maps and select Edit profile, then Booking. Select Add link, paste the address of the page where customers can finish a booking, and select Save. Google checks the link and can remove it if it breaks its rules. Every step and rule below links to Google's own help pages, so you can check the source yourself.

Before you start: what you need

You need a verified Business Profile and a web page where a customer can actually finish a booking. Google says "your business must be verified before you can add a link" (Google Business Profile Help). The page you link to has to work as a booking page, because "local business links must allow customers to complete the designated action" (Google Business Profile Help).

Not every business gets the option. Google says "not all businesses are eligible for business links," and "some countries and regions aren't eligible for certain business links" (Google Business Profile Help). You also add the link by hand: "local business links are not available via the Business Profile API or spreadsheet upload" (Google Business Profile Help).

A booking link works best on a complete profile. Google says "businesses with complete and accurate info are more likely to show up in local search results" (Google Business Profile Help). Our Google Business Profile audit checklist covers verification and core info step by step.

How to add a booking link on Google Search

On Google Search, you add a booking link from the Booking option on your profile (Google Business Profile Help):

  1. Go to your Business Profile.
  2. Select Booking.
  3. Select Add link, or Add another link if you already have one.
  4. Enter the address (URL) of your booking page.
  5. Select Save.

How to add a booking link in Google Maps

In Google Maps, you add a booking link through Edit profile (Google Business Profile Help):

  1. Open your Business Profile in Google Maps.
  2. Select Edit profile.
  3. Select the transaction type, Booking.
  4. Select Add link, or Add another link if you already have one.
  5. Enter the address (URL) of your booking page.
  6. Select Save.

How to set your preferred booking link

If your profile has more than one booking link, you can choose which one Google shows first (Google Business Profile Help):

  1. Select the transaction type, Booking.
  2. Select Set preferred link.
  3. Choose the link you want customers to see first.
  4. Select Save.

The preferred link shows at the top of the list, marked "Business preferred" (Google Business Profile Help).

Your own link or a Reserve with Google provider?

You can either add your own booking page as a link, or connect a booking provider through Reserve with Google. The differences Google points out are reporting and cost.

Your own link

Your own link is the page you add with the steps above, such as a booking page on your website. Google notes one trade-off: "performance data isn't available for custom links" (Google Business Profile Help).

A booking provider

A booking provider is an outside booking service that connects to Google. Google says "you can find providers available in your country through Reserve with Google," and "your booking providers will show up on your Business Profile within a week" (Google Business Profile Help). Check the cost first: "when you book through Google, your provider might charge you" (Google Business Profile Help).

Google's booking-link rules, as a checklist

Google's booking-link rules decide whether your link stays on your profile, so check each one before you save. Every rule below comes from Google's Business links policies & guidelines.

  • The link goes to a dedicated landing page for your business, not a general page, such as a homepage where customers can't book. Google says "local business links must lead to a dedicated landing page for your business," and a business with several locations must link to the specific location.
  • A customer can finish the booking on that page. Google says "local business links must allow customers to complete the designated action."
  • The link isn't a social media page, a messaging link, an app store link or a link shortener.
  • The page needs no login and no CAPTCHA, doesn't block or rate-limit Google's link checker, and isn't geoblocked. Google also notes that "these business link verification crawlers don't follow robots.txt rules."
  • The page loads fully and returns "a '200 OK' status code or a similar successful code," not an error such as 404, 403, 500 or 503.
  • You add only one link per domain. Google says "you can only add one link per domain," and it rejects a duplicate of a link that you or a provider already entered.
  • Google sets limits on how many links you can add.
  • The address has no spaces, stray symbols or backslashes, and no doubled protocol such as http://http://. Google shows these as "Invalid business link" errors.

We also test every booking link the way a customer would, which is our own practice rather than a Google rule. Tap the Book button on a phone while signed out, go all the way to a confirmed booking, then cancel it.

What if you take jobs by estimate, not by appointment?

If you take jobs by estimate, your booking page can book the estimate visit itself. This is our own advice, not a Google rule. Customers pick a time for the estimate, and you quote the job after the visit.

Keep your website and your Google profile offering the same thing. A customer who taps Book on Google should find the same booking option on your website, not a different form or a different tool.

Google's guidance on AI search mentions booking too. Google describes AI agents that act for people, "such as booking a reservation or comparing product specifications" (Google Search Central). Our guide on how to get your business to show up in AI search covers what Google says about AI search.

Troubleshooting

I don't see a Booking option

A missing Booking option can mean the profile isn't verified yet or the business isn't eligible. Google says "your business must be verified before you can add a link," and "not all businesses are eligible for business links" (Google Business Profile Help). Eligibility can also depend on where you are: "some countries and regions aren't eligible for certain business links" (Google Business Profile Help).

If your whole profile isn't showing up on Google, see Google Business Profile not showing up in search? What to check first.

My link was removed

Google can remove a link its tools can't reach. Google says "checks will be performed daily at most" and "links that cannot be crawled by our automated tools may be removed" (Google Business Profile Help). Check that the page doesn't need a login or CAPTCHA, doesn't block Google's checker, loads fully and returns a "200 OK" status (Google Business Profile Help).

There's a booking link I didn't add

A booking link you didn't add probably came from a partner or from Google's own data. Google says "links to certain services can automatically appear on your Business Profile on Google Search and Maps," from third-party partners or automated data (Google Business Profile Help). To remove one:

  • On Google Search, select Booking, then Remove Provider.
  • In Google Maps, select Edit profile, then Booking, then Remove Provider.

Google says "providers need to remove third-party links from your profile within 5 days of receiving a removal request" (Google Business Profile Help).

My change hasn't shown up yet

Google says edits to business info "can take up to 3 days to appear" (Google Business Profile Help). Google doesn't give a separate time for booking links. For a booking provider, Google says providers "will show up on your Business Profile within a week" (Google Business Profile Help).

Frequently asked questions

Why don't I see a Booking option on my Google Business Profile?

Google only shows the option to verified, eligible businesses. Google says "your business must be verified before you can add a link" and "not all businesses are eligible for business links" (Google Business Profile Help).

Can I use my homepage or my Facebook page as the booking link?

Not unless customers can finish a booking on that page. Google says "local business links must lead to a dedicated landing page for your business," and the links can't be social media sites, messaging links, app store links or link shorteners (Google Business Profile Help).

Why did Google remove my booking link?

Google can remove links its automated tools can't crawl, such as pages behind a login or CAPTCHA, geoblocked pages, or pages that don't return a "200 OK" status (Google Business Profile Help).

What's the difference between my own booking link and a Reserve with Google provider?

Your own link is a page you add yourself, and Google says "performance data isn't available for custom links" (Google Business Profile Help). A provider connects through Reserve with Google, shows up "within a week," and "might charge you" (Google Business Profile Help).

How do I remove a booking link I didn't add?

Select Booking, then Remove Provider, on Google Search or under Edit profile in Google Maps. Google says providers must remove the link "within 5 days of receiving a removal request" (Google Business Profile Help).

How long does it take for a booking link to show up?

Google says edits to business info "can take up to 3 days to appear" (Google Business Profile Help). Google doesn't give a separate time for booking links. A booking provider shows up "within a week" (Google Business Profile Help).
