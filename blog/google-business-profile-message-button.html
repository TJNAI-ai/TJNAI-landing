The new Message button on your Google listing: set it up or turn it off

By TJNAI · November 9, 2026 · 5 min read

Google just made messaging one of the first things a customer sees on your Maps listing. On Android and iOS, a Message button now sits right next to Call. Before this change, finding chat meant scrolling deep into the profile. As Google put it in its Nov 5, 2026 Small Business Bulletin (reported by Search Engine Journal): "Now, whether a customer views your profile, opens the expanded header, or uses the bottom action bar, messaging is front and center."

That helps if someone on your team will answer. It hurts if the button points at a dead link or an inbox nobody checks. Search Engine Journal notes that a dead link or unread inbox now sits in one of the top buttons on the listing, and recommends sending a test message through every profile's chat link before the holiday rush.

Below: what the button does, who can turn it on, how to add or clear it, and a short test before you leave it live. Facts come from Google's help pages and SEJ's report of Google's bulletin — not promises about leads, rankings, or results.

What the Message button actually does

The Message button only shows when chat is set up on the profile. When a customer taps it, Google sends them to whatever chat option the business configured: WhatsApp or text message.

Messages do not live inside the Google Business Profile itself. Since July 31, 2024, Google retired the old built-in Business Profile chat and call history. New messages go to the business's own WhatsApp or text inbox.

The button is a front door. The conversation happens in the WhatsApp or text inbox you already use.

Who can add it

Businesses with a claimed and verified Business Profile can add a WhatsApp or text-message option to their contact info.

Not every profile gets the option. Google says chat options are available in select regions and might not be available for every profile. If you do not see Chat in your contact settings, that may be why. This article cannot name which regions qualify — Google's help page in our brief does not list them.

How to add, edit, or clear chat

From a verified Business Profile:

  1. Open your Business Profile and choose Edit profile.
  2. Open Contact.
  3. Next to Chat, select Edit.
  4. Pick WhatsApp or Text message.
  5. Enter your WhatsApp click-to-chat URL, or a number that can receive texts.
  6. Select Save.

Google's help page covers how to add or edit Chat; it does not spell out a remove button. To clear or change what customers see, use the same Chat field under Contact: edit the option, clear the URL or number you do not want, and save.

One more rule that trips people up: if you add both WhatsApp and text, customers only see the text option. Pick the channel you will actually watch, or expect text to win.

A practical decision: set it up, or leave it off

Here is the call I would make for a local service business — a salon, a plumber, a shop with a front desk.

Set it up and test it if someone will answer WhatsApp or texts during the hours you serve customers. Treat Message like Call: leave it on only if a real person (or a system you trust) is on the other end.

Leave chat off if nobody owns the inbox. An unanswered Message button is worse than no button. The customer already decided to reach out; a silent thread is a cold stop.

That is judgment, not a Google ranking rule. Google does not require chat. The risk SEJ flags is practical: a top-of-listing button that goes nowhere.

Checklist before you leave the button live

Use this only if each step matches what you can verify yourself:

  • The profile is claimed and verified (required before chat can be added).
  • You chose one channel you will watch: WhatsApp or text (if both are saved, customers see text only).
  • The WhatsApp click-to-chat URL opens the account you mean to use.
  • The text option uses a number that can receive texts, and someone on your team can read that inbox.
  • You know who gets the messages during open hours (and who covers after hours, if anyone).
  • You sent a test message from a phone that is not the business line, tapped Message on your own Maps listing, and confirmed the thread arrived.
  • If the test fails, clear or change Chat in the same Contact field (Google Help covers edit, not a separate remove step) until it works — or leave chat off.

Search Engine Journal's holiday-rush advice is the same idea in one line: send a test through every profile's chat link before the busy season.

What this is not

This is not a ranking tip. Google's placement change makes messaging easier to find; it does not say the Message button improves local pack position. I am also skipping Google's AI messaging pilots and Workspace / Gemini access for Business Profiles — those sit outside this topic.

If chat is not on your profile, keep the rest of the listing accurate and complete, and check back later. Availability can depend on region and profile.
