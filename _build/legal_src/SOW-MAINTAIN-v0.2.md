## 2. Scope (what TJNAI will do each month)

1. **Regular monitoring checks.** TJNAI runs regular monitoring checks on the covered systems built under SOW-BUILD (for example: the Google Business Profile listing is live and its hours are correct; review requests are still sending; lead-form submissions arrive and follow-up fires; the booking link works and the calendar is connected; the weekly owner email was sent). These are periodic checks, not 24/7 or uptime monitoring.
2. **Monthly report.** A one-page report sent in the first week of each month: what was checked, what broke and was fixed, review and lead counts pulled from Client's own tools, and one suggested improvement.
3. **Tweaks.** Up to **2 small changes per month**, together totaling **no more than 1 hour** of work (for example: updating hours, wording, a form field, an automatic message or booking availability). Unused tweaks and time do not roll over. Anything larger needs a Change Order approved by Tim.
4. **Fixes to TJNAI's own setup.** If something TJNAI built stops working because of TJNAI's setup, TJNAI fixes it at no extra charge, even beyond the tweak allowance.
5. **Third-party problems.** If a covered system stops working because of a Third-Party Platform (for example, Google or the booking tool), TJNAI will tell Client and try to work around it within the tweak allowance; it does not guarantee a fix (Section 4).
