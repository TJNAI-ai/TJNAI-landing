1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how tjnai.ai LLC [B22 · Tim: confirm exact registered name] (doing business as TJNAI), a North Carolina limited liability company ("TJNAI," "we," "us"), collects, uses and shares information.

Mailing address: 5540 Centerview Dr Ste 204, PMB #588341, Raleigh, NC 27606-8012. Email: info@tjnai.ai.

2. Words with a special meaning

  • Site: the website at tjnai.ai.
  • Services: our Audit, Build and Care Plan services, as defined in our Terms of Service.
  • Client: a business that buys Services from us.
  • Personal Information: information that identifies, or can reasonably be linked to, a particular person.
  • Client Data: information a Client, or a Client's accounts, gives us so we can perform Services. It can include Personal Information about the Client's staff and the Client's own customers.
  • Service Provider: a company that processes information for us to help run our business or deliver Services.

3. Where this policy applies

This policy covers the Site, emails with us, payments, and Services. Our Services are offered to businesses in the United States. The Site and Services are not designed for people outside the United States.

This policy is a notice. It is not a contract, except where the law makes it one.

4. What we collect and why

4.1 Visiting the Site

  • No cookies, analytics or tracking. The Site does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from tjnai.ai. The Site has no forms that collect visitor information. [B13 · Eva/Tim: confirm this is still true before the Site reopens]
  • Hosting logs. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical log data, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, as part of hosting. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Domain and DNS. Our domain and DNS are run by GoDaddy. When a device looks up tjnai.ai, the lookup is handled by GoDaddy's DNS servers.
  • Google Search Console. We verify our domain with Google Search Console through a DNS record. Search Console gives us information about how the Site appears in Google search. It does not place anything on the Site.

4.2 Emailing us

If you email info@tjnai.ai, we receive your name, email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it to reply and to keep a record of our conversation. Our email runs on Microsoft 365 (Exchange Online).

4.3 Payments

  • Services are paid through Stripe. Digital products are sold and delivered through Gumroad. Payments happen on their sites, not on tjnai.ai, and nothing payment-related loads on the Site.
  • We receive payment details such as your name, business name, email, billing address, the amount, and the date. We do not receive or store full card numbers.
  • Stripe and Gumroad handle payment information under their own privacy policies.

4.4 Accepting our terms

When a Client accepts our documents at checkout, we record who accepted, the document ID and version, the date and time, the exact checkbox text, the IP address and the browser or device information. We use this to prove what was agreed. Checkout acceptance is handled by a small program we run on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers), and the acceptance records are stored in Cloudflare's database service (D1) in our Cloudflare account. Cloudflare processes this data for us as a Service Provider under its own terms and privacy policy. [B23 · Eva: confirm data location; D1 region is not pinned. Do not state a US-only location unless confirmed]

7. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, to keep records of our engagements and agreements (including acceptance records), and to meet legal, tax and accounting duties. Then we delete it.

After an engagement ends, we delete or return Client Data on the Client's written request (MSA Section 11.6), except copies in routine backups or copies we must keep by law, and we remove our access to the Client's accounts. Acceptance records are kept for [6 years — Tim's setting, attorney to confirm] after the customer relationship ends, because they are our proof of what was agreed. GitHub's hosting logs are kept under GitHub's own policies; we do not control them.

8. How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards for information we hold. We access Client accounts through roles the Client grants and can revoke, not shared passwords. We limit access to the people and providers who need it. No system is perfectly secure.

If we learn of a security breach affecting Personal Information we hold, we will notify the affected Client, and affected people where the law requires, without unreasonable delay and as applicable law requires (for example, North Carolina's security-breach law, N.C. Gen. Stat. § 75-65).

9. Your choices and requests

  • Requests. You can ask us to tell you what Personal Information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email info@tjnai.ai or write to our mailing address. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will respond as the law requires.
  • Client Data. If we hold your information because a Client (for example, a business you are a customer of) gave it to us, we will pass your request to that Client and help it respond.
  • State laws. Some states give their residents additional privacy rights. We do not claim that any particular state's comprehensive privacy law applies to us. If one does, we will honor the rights it gives.
  • Email. You can ask us to stop sending you marketing email at any time [B14: and use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter, if launched].

10. Children

Our Site and Services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from children under 13. If you believe we have, email info@tjnai.ai and we will delete it.
