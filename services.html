How it works

Three steps: audit, build, care plan

The audit maps where you lose time and leads. The build sets up the best fixes. The care plan keeps them running, if you want it.

Step 1

The audit

$350

Paid upfront · results in one week

  • About 3 business days of team work, delivered within the week.
  • Pre-audit research: your website (contact form, speed to answer) and how leads get captured.
  • Google Business Profile snapshot: claimed or not, reviews, hours and photos.
  • A 30-minute intake call with Tim on where you lose time and leads.
  • A written map with the top 3–5 fixes recommended and ranked. Tim approves it and walks you through it.

If you go ahead with a build, your $350 audit fee is credited toward it.

Step 2

The build

$750 total

10 business days

  • 2–3 fixes from your audit, picked from the build menu below.
  • Set up, connected and tested for you, plus a short how-to guide.
  • Ends with a handoff walkthrough with Tim.

Your $350 audit is credited, so the remaining $400 is paid as $200 upfront and $200 at handoff.

Step 3

Care plan

$150 / month

Optional, after handoff

  • Monitoring
  • Tweaks
  • A monthly report

Offered at handoff. You choose whether to add it.

The build menu

What the build can include

Your audit recommends which of these fit your business. The build covers 2–3 of them.

  1. AGoogle profile fixes, plus an automatic review request after each job.
  2. BAn AI front desk that drafts replies to website, email and FAQ inquiries. You approve the replies.
  3. CA lead capture form with automatic follow-up.
  4. DA weekly summary email to you, the owner.

Any AI or software service in your build runs on your own accounts, and you pay those providers directly. We don’t host, resell or mark them up. You pay us only for the build and, if you want it, the care plan.
