2. Scope (what TJNAI will do)

  1. Outside research of Client's public presence before the intake call: the Google Business Profile Snapshot (claimed or not, reviews, hours, photos), Client's website (contact form, speed to answer) and how leads are captured.
  2. Intake call: one video or phone call of up to 30 minutes about where Client loses time and leads.
  3. Findings and report: a written audit report with the top 3–5 recommended fixes, and which 2–3 of them could be built in a Build engagement.
  4. Presentation: TJNAI walks Client through the written Audit Report on a walkthrough call (video or phone).

3. Deliverables

  • One audit report (PDF or shared document) — the "Audit Report."
  • One walkthrough call presenting the Audit Report.

4. Exclusions (not included)

  • Building, configuring or changing anything in Client Accounts (that is a Build).
  • Logging into Client Accounts, unless Client chooses to grant view access.
  • Legal, tax, accessibility, security-penetration, or regulatory-compliance review.
  • Paid advertising management, SEO guarantees, review solicitation or responses.
  • Additional calls, locations or revisions beyond Section 8.

5. Assumptions

  • The research uses publicly available information and what Client provides. TJNAI does not verify the accuracy of third-party data (for example, Google listing data).
  • Findings reflect conditions on the dates checked; Third-Party Platforms change often.
  • One business and [one] location unless stated in Section 1.

6. Client inputs

  • Book and attend the intake call within [5] Business Days of the up-front payment.
  • Answer follow-up questions within [2] Business Days.
  • Tell TJNAI about any tools, vendors or constraints that matter (CRM, phone system, booking tools).

7. Timeline
