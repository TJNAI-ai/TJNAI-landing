2. Definitions

  • Agreement: this MSA plus every SOW accepted under it, and the documents they incorporate.
  • SOW (Statement of Work): a document for one engagement that describes the Services, Deliverables, fees and timeline. The current templates are SOW-AUDIT, SOW-BUILD and SOW-MAINTAIN.
  • Standard Package: an AI Agent Audit, a Build of 2 to 3 items from the menu in SOW-BUILD, or the Maintain monthly care plan, each on the unmodified SOW template at its listed price.
  • Custom Build: any engagement whose scope, price, payment schedule or legal terms differ from a Standard Package (including a Build with items outside the SOW-BUILD menu or more than 3 items).
  • Services: the work TJNAI performs under an SOW (Audit, Build, or Maintain, the monthly care plan).
  • Deliverables: the specific items an SOW says TJNAI will hand over (for example, an audit report, a configured workflow, a how-to guide, a monthly report).
  • TJNAI Materials: everything TJNAI owned, developed or licensed before or independently of the SOW, or develops for general use, including tools, templates, prompts, scripts, workflows, checklists, methods, know-how, code libraries and documentation patterns, and improvements to them.
  • Client Content: data, text, images, logos, trademarks, customer lists, credentials, instructions and other material Client (or someone on Client's behalf) provides or makes available to TJNAI.
  • Client Accounts: accounts, platforms, software and services that Client owns, controls or subscribes to (for example, Google Business Profile, website host, email, CRM, phone, booking, payment, AI or automation tools).
  • Third-Party Platform: any product, service, API, plugin, model or platform not owned by TJNAI, whether used by Client or by TJNAI to provide Services.
  • Fees: the amounts stated in an SOW.
  • Work Start: for an Audit, when TJNAI begins the outside research after the up-front payment clears; for a Build, when TJNAI begins building after the up-front payment clears (Day 2 of the SOW-BUILD plan). TJNAI will tell Client by email when Work Start happens.
  • Prepayment: any amount Client paid for an Audit or Build before TJNAI performed the work it pays for.
  • Unused Prepayment: the part of a Prepayment that pays for work not yet started when a job stops. The Audit credit is not Unused Prepayment.
  • Job stops: an Audit or Build ends before it is finished. A pause while TJNAI waits on Client (Section 4.4) is not a stop.
  • Change Order: a written change to an SOW's scope, timeline or Fees, approved under Section 8.
  • Business Day: Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays.
  • Effective Date: the date Client first accepts this MSA (electronically or by signature).
